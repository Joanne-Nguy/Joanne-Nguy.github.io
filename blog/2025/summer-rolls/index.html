summer rolls with pork

Created on April 30, 2025

2025   ·   cooking,   savory,   sweet   ·   recipes

Abstract

A recipe for deliciously simple rice paper rolls with versatile topping choices for a fun hands-on meal with creamy peanut dipping sauce on the side.

Materials

  • Freezer bag
  • Saute pan
  • Saucepan
  • Silicone spatula
  • Colander
  • Wide bowl
  • 3 Plates

Reagents

Meat and marinade

||Ingredient|Amount|Preparation| |:—|:—|:—|:—| ||Water|2 Cups|None| ||Soy sauce|1 Cup|None| ||Fish Sauce|2 Tbsp|None| ||Black pepper flakes|1 Tbsp|None| ||Optional: Cayenne|1 Tbsp|None| ||Neutral oil|1 Tbsp|None| ||Pork neck or loin|2 lbs|Thinly sliced, fresh or frozen|


Dipping Sauce

||Ingredient|Amount|Preparation| |:—|:—|:—|:—| ||Crunchy peanut butter|1/2 cup|May be substituted with regular peanut butter and coarsely chopped peanuts| ||Lime juice or rice vinegar|1 Tbsp|None| ||Optional: Sambal|1 Tbsp|None|


Fresh toppings

||Ingredient|Amount|Preparation| |:—|:—|:—|:—| ||Vermicelli noodles|1 serving|None| ||Rice paper|1 packet of at least 8|None| ||Do chua||None| ||Lettuce|2 small heads|Washed and dried| ||Mint|3-5 sprigs|Washed and dried| ||Thai basil|3-5 sprigs|Washed and dried| ||Cucumber|1/2 cucumber|Washed and cut into matchsticks| ||Bell pepper|1 pepper|Washed and cut into matchsticks| ||Optional: Green mango|1 mango|Washed and cut into matchsticks|


Procedure

Marinate pork

Hands-on: 10 mins| Incubation: 1-12 h

  1. Add marinade ingredients to a freezer bag and gently shake to mix.
  2. Add thinly sliced pork to the freezer bag.
  3. Mix pork and marinade to ensure even coating.
  4. Allow meat to marinate for at least 1 hour to overnight in the fridge.
    • If marinating frozen meat, allow additional time for defrosting, at least 4 hours.
Cook pork

Hands-on: 20 mins

  1. Reserve 4 Tbsp of marinade for the peanut sauce. The rest may be poured out or reserved for another recipe.
  2. Heat saute pan to medium-heat. Add neutral cooking oil.
  3. Cook pork slices in small batches depending on pan size to prevent crowding the pan.
  4. Cook pork slice for 1 min before flipping and cooking the other side for 30s.
  5. Place finished pork slices in a plate.
Peanut sauce
  1. Add reserved marinade to a saucepan and bring to a boil.
  2. Add peanut butter and stir to mix. Sauce will be thick.
  3. Turn off the heat.
  4. Add lime juice and sambal if using.
  5. Scoop sauce into small dipping bowls.
Cook Vermicelli
  1. Bring 4 cups of water to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Add vermicelli noodles to the boiling water and cook for 8 mins or as indicated by the package.
  3. Place colander over the sink. Pour cooked vermicelli into the colander, allowing the water to drain.
  4. Briefly rinse the cooked vermicelli with cold water, tossing to ensure even cooling.
  5. Place the colander over a plate.
Assembling the Summer Rolls
  1. Add water to the wide bowl.
  2. Roll a piece of rice paper 2 times (720o), ensuring there are no dry spots on the paper.
  3. Place the rice paper on your plate.
  4. Place a lettuce leaf in the bottom third of the rice paper. This will serve as the backbone for your roll.
  5. Add vermicelli, the cooked pork, do chua, mint, basil, cucumber, bell pepper and any additional desired ingredients in the lettuce basket. Don’t overstuff your roll or it will fall apart!
  6. Grab the bottom edge of the rice paper and pull it over the ingredients, tightly covering them.
  7. Fold in the left and right edges to seal in the ingredients.
  8. Finish rolling the rice paper tightly. The rice paper will seal the roll by itself.
  9. Your finished summer roll is ready to be dipped in the peanut sauce and enjoyed!
Yield 8 rolls.
Storage Wrap any leftover rolls individually in cling wrap. Store in the fridge for up to 3 days.


Discussion

Summer rolls with a side of peanut sauce. Photo from Unsplash.

Summer rolls are an easy way to introduce friends and family to the simple freshness of Vietnamese cuisine. The simple art of building your own roll adds a touch of personalization that caters to all taste buds. In this recipe, I mix a combination of fresh, crunchy and chewy textures which all come together in a neat little package to be dipped in a crunchy, nutty sauce.

Protein Variations

In this recipe, sauteed pork is used as the protein. However, feel free to substitute with other proteins. Some traditional options are shrimp, BBQ meat and tofu strips. The protein not only provides additional nutritional value, but serves as the backbone of the flavor profile and texture. The chewiness of the protein complements the other textures in the roll.

Topping Variations

Most of the fresh toppings add a bit of crunch to the roll to create a fun and exciting mouthfeel. Other toppings to try are carrot matchsticks, cilantro, bean sprouts, purple cabbage, radishes, pickled mushrooms, bok choy or avocado; ordered from traditional to more creative. Summer rolls are especially refreshing when making them similar to how one would pair fruit salads, with plenty of mangoes, peaches, watermelon, pear, cucumber and mint.

Most importantly, use ingredients that are available to you either in the markets or your own garden.

Dipping Sauce Variations

My preferred dipping sauce is a thick peanut sauce that can be scooped with my summer rolls - it especially goes well with savory proteins. For a lighter profile, a traditional fish sauce blend may be used instead, mixing a 1:1:2 ratio of fish sauce:sugar:water and adding a splash of vinegar or do chua for brightness. This variation is more runny than the peanut sauce so be mindful of drips!
