#### Abstract

A recipe for a fermented hot honey with a spicy habanero kick and pungent aromatics.

#### Materials

- **Recommended**: food-safe powder-free gloves
- Knife and cutting board
- Glass canning jar
- Saucepan

#### Reagents

||Ingredient|Amount|Preparation|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|<input type="checkbox">|Habanero|1/8 cup|None|
|<input type="checkbox">|Thai chili|1/4 cup|None|
|<input type="checkbox">|Garlic|8 cloves|Sliced|
|<input type="checkbox">|Shallots|1 shallot|Sliced|
|<input type="checkbox">|Distilled Vinegar|1/4 cup|None|
|<input type="checkbox">|**Optional**: Thyme|3-5 sprigs|None|

<br>

#### Procedure

##### Prepare Peppers
**Hands-on**: 5 mins
1. Don gloves, wash gloves with soap.
2. Using a knife and cutting board, slice habaneros and Thai chilies in half. Remove seeds and stem.
3. Slice peppers into thin strips - the thinner, the faster the ferment.
4. Set aside in a small bowl. Discard gloves.

##### Prepare Hot Honey
**Hands-on**: 10 mins | **Incubation**: 5-14 days
1. Add habaneros, Thai chilies, garlic, shallots, distilled vinegar and thyme, if using, in a glass jar.
2. Fill jar to the top with raw honey.
3. Allow to ferment for 5-14 days. 
- Stir with a sterilized spoon daily until mixture becomes runny. Water content from the ingredients are being drawn out and into the honey.
- Small bubbles will form to indicate that fermentation is occurring.

##### Preserve Hot Honey
**Hands-on**: 10 mins | **Incubation**: 2+ days
1. Add jar to a pot and completely submerge with hot water. Boil for 5-10 mins depending on altitude.
2. Remove from heat.
3. Test seal by pressing the center of the lid. If lid springs back, repeat boiling process.
4. Allow jar to cool to room temperature, about 12 h.
